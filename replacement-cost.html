Methodology
Replacement cost = land acquisition + build cost + other costs + developer profit, expressed per m² of floor area, then compared against the established median to give a discount (a positive discount means it is cheaper to buy established than to build new).
Build cost/m² = anchor cost × (Rawlinsons regional multiplier / 100) × (ABS construction index / index anchor). The ABS index handles time and cross-capital cost level; the Rawlinsons multiplier handles sub-capital spatial premium. National weighted-average anchors: house $2,358, unit $4,225; index anchor 163.3.
Ring multipliers scale land value for inner and middle rings, calibrated from CoreLogic-style vacant-land median $/m² sales on realestate.com.au (simple median per ring; multiplier = ring median / outer median). Outer = 1.0 by definition. Houses scale the whole lot; units scale a 100m² land share.
Single-market regions show one figure where vacant-land sampling did not support a defensible ring gradient (flat, thin, or inverted) — the calibration doubles as a test of whether a market is multi-centred enough for rings.
