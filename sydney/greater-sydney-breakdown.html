Method and sources

  • Districts
    The five districts of the NSW Government’s Greater Sydney Region Plan, plus Central Coast as a sixth. 34 LGAs = the ABS Greater Sydney GCCSA. The compass names are ours: the published names are Eastern City, Central City and Western City, and North District and South District are the only two we have kept verbatim. Central Coast is not a Region Plan district at all — it sits in the ABS statistical area and is carried separately.
  • Every page, every district
    There is no district or segment selector. Segment-dependent exhibits show houses and units together — solid and dashed within a district panel, or paired series in a bar chart. Two exhibits use a 12-panel district-by-segment grid.
  • Interaction
    Hover any chart for a crosshair and a readout of every visible series. Click a legend entry, a table row name, a map region or a confidence column header to hide that district everywhere; Show all restores. Table headers sort.
  • Price level, not median
    A district is not a CoreLogic geography, so no district median is published. The figure shown is the sales-weighted average of member-LGA medians, each of which is a real CoreLogic median computed from transactions. Reconstructing a true district median from the Suburbs tab was tested and rejected: validated against published LGA medians it fell within ±2% for only 14 of 32 LGAs and erred by up to 16.3% (Woollahra), because suburb medians are themselves small-sample medians. The min–max range on every card is the honest signal of internal spread.
  • Composite figures
    Sales-weighted for medians, DOM, discount and growth; rental-listing-weighted for rents; stock-weighted for yield, stock on market and hold period; simple sums for counts.
  • Price position
    Ranked inside the 34-LGA distribution, not against the five sibling districts — a field of six cannot support percentile scoring.
  • Colour and dash
    Each district has a fixed colour and dash pattern, with lines end-labelled in dark text, so no exhibit relies on colour alone. Luminance is spread across the six so they separate in greyscale.
  • Reference bands
    Growth and correction periods sit behind the Price level exhibit, as in the printed report.
  • Live data
    CoreLogic / Cotality Market Trends, LGA tab, May 2026. Boundaries: ABS ASGS 2025 LGA generalised layer, dissolved by district at 4% simplification.
  • Synthetic
    Every value marked with a degree sign — vacancy rates, all pillar scores, runway, confidence indicators, and the 2006–2026 history. Seeded, reproducible, meaningless.
  • Still required
    NSW LGA VR & Rent files; ABS 3218.0 ×3; ABS 8731.0; Census G02; NAB NSW; ABS Lending Indicators; NSW underemployment; JSA SALM; JSA IVI plus a district crosswalk; a 36-month CoreLogic series pull.
  • Region plan provenance
    Published by the Greater Sydney Commission, March 2018. That body became the Greater Cities Commission and was dissolved on 1 January 2024; the district plans remain statutory instruments under the EP&A Act.
